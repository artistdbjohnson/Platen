# Mordvinian & Near Eastern Palette Guide

Based on the `larkspur-quine.html` codebase, the following **Chrome** options represent traditional Mordvinian embroidery colors and ancient Near Eastern dyes.

## Authentic Mordvinian Colors

The traditional Mordvinian style is characterized by heavy use of red embroidery on a white or hemp canvas, often outlined in black.

*   **`rutsya` (Red)**
    *   **Description:** The most traditional and authentic Mordvinian color.
    *   **Dominant Colors:** Vermillion, brick red, and dark sienna. To capture the true spirit of Mordvinian embroidery, this is the primary choice.

*   **`kelme` (Ink/Black)**
    *   **Description:** Represents black sheep wool or dark ink.
    *   **Aesthetic:** High contrast and graphic. Often used for outlines or distinct geometric patterns in traditional work.

*   **`panar` (Canvas)**
    *   **Description:** The natural colors of the hemp or parchment cloth itself.
    *   **Dominant Colors:** Gold, warm brown, and umber.

## Ancient Dye Colors

These palettes are inspired by historical dyes from the Near East.

*   **`viride` (Green Earth)**
    *   **Description:** Forest green and olive tones.
    *   **Note:** Historically an accent color, but in this palette, it takes center stage.

*   **`tekhelet` (Indigo Blue)**
    *   **Description:** Deep slate and midnight blues.
    *   **History:** An ancient dye famously used in textiles. Great for a deep, moody aesthetic similar to Viride but in blue.

*   **`karkom` (Saffron Gold)**
    *   **Description:** Warm ceremonial golds and ochres.
    *   **Aesthetic:** Rich and vibrant, often associated with royalty or ceremony.

*   **`argaman` (Tyrian Purple)**
    *   **Description:** Rich wine and crimson hues.
    *   **History:** A precious dye typically reserved for high status.

*   **`madder` (Rubia Root)**
    *   **Description:** Bricky reds and dusty rose tones.
    *   **History:** A common plant-based red dye, softer than the brilliant *Rutsya* red.

*   **`murex` (Sea Snail)**
    *   **Description:** Crimson mixed with midnight blue.
    *   **History:** Another variant of the Tyrian purple dye extraction.

*   **`kokkos` (Scarlet Worm)**
    *   **Description:** Bright scarlet.
    *   **History:** A brilliant red dye derived from insects (kermes).

## Recommendation

If you enjoy the **`viride`** (Green) palette because of its deep, single-color dominance, you might also enjoy **`tekhelet`** (Blue) or **`karkom`** (Gold).

## Modern Interpretations (Viride Style)

These palettes follow your preference for a strong, single-color dominance with high-contrast darks.

*   **`shahar` (Dawn)**
    *   **Dominant Color:** Salmon / Pink.
    *   **Aesthetic:** Warm, soft, and glowing like the morning sky.

*   **`gehelet` (Ember)**
    *   **Dominant Color:** Burnt Umber.
    *   **Aesthetic:** Deep, rich earth tones, like glowing coals in a fire.

*   **`tarshish` (Beryl / Sea Stone)**
    *   **Dominant Color:** Aqua / Teal.
    *   **Aesthetic:** Cool, refreshing, and jewel-like, similar to the ancient stone *Tarshish*.
