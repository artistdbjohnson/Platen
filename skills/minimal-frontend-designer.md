# Minimal Frontend Designer
## Academic Minimalism / Editorial Typographic Modernism

This skill encodes the design language of cultural institutions, luxury editorial studios, and
Swiss typographic tradition — applied to frontend UI.

**Lineage**: Swiss International Typographic Style → Jan Tschichold → Emigre → Experimental Jetset →
Contemporary: OK-RM, Norm (Zurich), Studio Frith, Bureau Borsche

---

## The Ten Laws

### Law 1 — Typography IS the Design
### Law 2 — Ornament is Failure
### Law 3 — The Active Void (40-60% empty space)
### Law 4 — Warm Neutrals Only, Zero Digital Colors
### Law 5 — Typographic Vocabulary (roman numerals, slash notation, em dash, parenthetical categories)
### Law 6 — Font System (two fonts only: JetBrains Mono + Cormorant Garamond)
### Law 7 — Object Photography Grammar (object on ground, clinical, documentary)
### Law 8A — The Gallery Hang (flush grid, no cards, no borders)
### Law 8B — The Card System (cream background, hairline border, editorial copy)
### Law 9 — Motion is Almost Zero (opacity fade only, 400ms+)
### Law 9B — Dark/Light Toggle (◻/◼, no icons)
### Law 10 — Grid is Structure (3-col desktop, 2-col tablet, 1-col mobile)
